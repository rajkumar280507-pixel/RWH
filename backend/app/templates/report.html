6. Construction Sequence

The following is a generic construction sequence for a rooftop-fed {{ design.structure_type | replace('_', ' ') }}, consistent with IS 15797:2008 (Roof Top Rain Water Harvesting — Guidelines) and the CGWB Master Plan for Artificial Recharge to Ground Water. Site-specific supervision by a qualified engineer is required; this sequence does not replace project-specific working drawings.

  1. Mark out structure location and verify setback from building foundation, boundary and any existing utilities/services.
  2. Excavate to the design depth and dimensions; keep excavated sides stable (shore/batter as required by soil type and depth).
  3. Verify the base of excavation is below the design invert level and reasonably level and compacted.
  4. Install the pre-filter / desilting chamber upstream of the structure so first-flush and coarse silt are intercepted before reaching the recharge zone.
  5. Place filter media in layers, coarsest at the bottom, finest near the inflow, per the filter media stack in Section 2 — each layer compacted lightly and kept clean of surrounding soil.
  6. Construct/lower lining (brick masonry ring / PCC rings / HDPE casing as specified) with weep holes or perforations sized per the design.
  7. Connect roof downpipes to the conveyance pipe network, sized per the hydraulic design, with a first-flush diverter fitted ahead of the structure inlet.
  8. Provide an overflow pipe set at the design freeboard level, directed to an approved safe disposal point (storm drain / soak zone).
  9. Cap/cover the structure with a removable, safety-compliant cover (RCC slab with inspection chamber, or equivalent) to allow future desilting access while preventing accidental entry.
  10. Backfill around the structure in layers, compacting as per good practice, and reinstate the surrounding surface.
  11. Commission: flush the system, confirm free drainage/infiltration, and record as-built dimensions against this design.

7. Technical Notes

  • Structure sizing follows the rational-method water balance (catchment area × rainfall × runoff coefficient), first-flush allowance and CGWB artificial-recharge efficiency factors used by the RWH-DSS design engine.
  • Filter media gradation and hydraulic conductivity values follow standard graded-filter practice for rooftop recharge structures (coarse aggregate/gravel/sand layering).
  • Where an injection borewell is indicated, it is a conceptual recommendation only — a licensed hydrogeologist/driller must confirm aquifer suitability, casing design and chlorination/development procedure before construction, per CGWB guidelines.
  • All dimensions in this report are as computed/stored by the design engine at design-creation time; site verification of levels, utilities and soil conditions is required before construction.
